### Entries with no exact HD match

These are the cases the bridge has to reason about. Every one is the
secondary component of a visual double or multiple that YBS lists as
its own HR record and Hipparcos publishes as a single combined
solution carrying only one component's HD.

The neighbour match is a merge, not a rename: the named secondary and
its primary resolve to the *same* HIP entry, so the two HR records
