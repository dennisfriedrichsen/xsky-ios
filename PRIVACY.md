# XSky Privacy Policy

XSky does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- The app makes no network connections. It has no accounts, analytics,
  advertising, tracking, or crash reporting.
- It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, or
  microphone.
- Your chart settings and any user catalogs you import are saved only on
  your device, and are removed when you delete the app.

Questions or problems: open an issue at
https://github.com/dennisfriedrichsen/xsky-ios/issues
